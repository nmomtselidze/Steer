A Vectorized PyTorch Implementation of the Frenet-Serret Formulas for 3D Curve GenerationNodar Momtselidze Tbilisi, Georgia Email: nodar.m@cybx.io ---AbstractThe generation and visualization of three-dimensional curves are foundational operations in differential geometry, computer graphics, and kinematics. Traditionally, numerical integration of the Frenet-Serret formulas is executed via sequential, scalar iterations. This approach inherently underutilizes modern parallel computing architectures and is prone to the accumulation of numerical drift. This paper presents a novel, vectorized computational framework implemented within PyTorch. By reformulating the Frenet-Serret frame as a system of coupled linear differential equations and introducing Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) for robust frame orthogonalization, we achieve a highly optimized, GPU-accelerated pipeline. The proposed method is validated against analytical solutions for canonical curves, demonstrating significant advantages in computational efficiency and geometric fidelity.1. This work introduces a native PyTorch optimization that reformulates the Frenet-Serret system into parallelized matrix differential equations, entirely eliminating sequential temporal dependencies and enabling direct GPU acceleration.2.
